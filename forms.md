# Konvo Club forms

Copy each question into Google Forms as written. Type and required flag are in brackets. Leave "Collect email addresses" off for both (WhatsApp is the main channel). Link each form to its own Google Sheet.

---

## Form 1: Join the Dean's Lens (photographers)

**Form title:** Join the Dean's Lens

**Form description:**

> Konvo Club pairs graduates with vetted photographers for their convocation. You shoot; we find the clients, take payment and handle the pricing chats.
>
> Free to join. No guaranteed jobs yet, and we're saying that upfront. Pass screening and you're on the Dean's Lens. If grads book, you're first in line.
>
> Takes about 5 minutes. Have a link to one full delivered gallery ready.

### Section 1: About you

1. **Full name** [Short answer, required]

2. **WhatsApp number** [Short answer, required]
   Help text: We'll message you here, not by email.
   Validation: Regular expression, matches `^\+?[0-9 \-]{9,15}$`, error text "Numbers only, e.g. 012 345 6789"

3. **Instagram handle** [Short answer, required]
   Help text: The account where you post your work, e.g. @yourname

4. **Where are you based?** [Short answer, required]
   Help text: Area is enough, e.g. Subang Jaya, Cheras, Puchong.

5. **Languages you can chat comfortably in with a family** [Checkboxes, required]
   - English
   - Bahasa Melayu
   - Mandarin
   - Cantonese
   - Tamil
   - Hokkien
   - Other: (enable "Other")

### Section 2: Your work

6. **What have you shot before?** [Checkboxes, required]
   - Graduations
   - Portraits
   - Events
   - Weddings or engagements
   - Family shoots
   - Other: (enable "Other")

7. **Roughly how many graduation shoots have you done?** [Multiple choice, required]
   - None yet
   - 1 to 5
   - 6 to 20
   - More than 20

8. **Link to one full delivered gallery from a real shoot** [Short answer, required]
   Help text: The whole set the client received (Google Drive, Pixieset, etc.), not your best 10. We're checking consistency, not highlights. Please make sure the link is viewable by anyone.

9. **Anything else we should look at?** [Short answer, optional]
   Help text: Portfolio site, a second gallery, a reel.

### Section 3: Gear

10. **Camera body (or bodies)** [Short answer, required]
    Help text: e.g. Sony A7 III, Canon R6

11. **Lenses you'd bring to a grad shoot** [Paragraph, required]
    Help text: e.g. 24-70mm f/2.8, 85mm f/1.8

12. **Do you carry a spare battery and a spare memory card on every shoot?** [Multiple choice, required]
    - Yes, both
    - Spare battery only
    - Spare card only
    - Not usually

13. **Do you have a flash or reflector for harsh midday light?** [Multiple choice, required]
    - Yes
    - No

### Section 4: Delivery and rates

14. **How long do you usually take to deliver edited photos?** [Multiple choice, required]
    - Within 3 days
    - 4 to 7 days
    - 1 to 2 weeks
    - More than 2 weeks

15. **What do you usually charge for a 2-hour grad shoot?** [Short answer, required]
    Help text: Your normal rate in RM. This won't be shown to anyone.

16. **We pay a fixed amount per shoot (grads pay us, we pay you once the gallery is delivered). Are you OK with a fixed payout?** [Multiple choice, required]
    - Yes
    - Depends on the amount
    - No, I only work at my own rate

17. **Which campuses can you get to?** [Checkboxes, required]
    - Sunway University / Monash Malaysia (Bandar Sunway)
    - Taylor's University (Lakeside)
    - UM (Kuala Lumpur)
    - UKM (Bangi)
    - UiTM Shah Alam
    - UPM (Serdang)
    - APU (Bukit Jalil)
    - UCSI (Cheras)
    - INTI / SEGi / other private colleges
    - Anywhere in Klang Valley
    - Other: (enable "Other")

### Section 5: Availability

18. **Which convocation seasons could you shoot?** [Checkboxes, required]
    - Taylor's, end of January 2027
    - Sunway, around February 2027
    - Monash, around April 2027
    - Sunway, around July 2027
    - Not sure yet

19. **Are you OK with weekday shoots?** [Multiple choice, required]
    Help text: A lot of ceremonies happen on weekdays.
    - Yes
    - Some weekdays
    - Weekends only

20. **Quick one: the grad's aunty wants 15 more group photos and you're out of time. What do you do?** [Paragraph, required]
    Help text: No right answer. We just want to hear how you handle families.

21. **Can we do a 10-minute call if your portfolio passes?** [Multiple choice, required]
    - Yes
    - Prefer WhatsApp voice note

**Confirmation message:**

> Thanks. We'll go through your full gallery and get back to you on WhatsApp within a week. If you pass, you're on the Dean's Lens.

## Form 2: Get matched (graduates)

**Form title:** Get matched with a Konvo Club photographer

**Form description:**

> Tell us about your convocation and we'll send you two vetted photographers that fit your style and budget, usually within 2 days. You pick one. No payment needed to get your shortlist.

### Section 1: You

1. **Your name** [Short answer, required]

2. **WhatsApp number** [Short answer, required]
   Help text: Your shortlist comes here.
   Validation: same regex as the photographer form.

3. **University** [Multiple choice, required]
   - Sunway University
   - Taylor's University
   - Monash University Malaysia
   - UM
   - UKM
   - UiTM
   - UPM
   - Other: (enable "Other")

### Section 2: The day

4. **Convocation date** [Date, required]
   Help text: Not confirmed yet? Pick your best guess and tell us below.

5. **Is the date confirmed?** [Multiple choice, required]
   - Yes
   - Not yet

6. **Your ceremony session, if you know it** [Short answer, optional]
   Help text: e.g. Session 3, 2pm. Helps us plan around it.

7. **When do you want photos taken?** [Multiple choice, required]
   - Before the ceremony
   - After the ceremony
   - Both
   - A different day on campus (gown photos)
   - Not sure, advise me

8. **How many people will be in the family/friends photos?** [Multiple choice, required]
   - Just me
   - 2 to 4
   - 5 to 10
   - More than 10

9. **What language does your family speak most comfortably?** [Checkboxes, required]
   Help text: We'll try to pair you with a photographer who speaks it.
   - English
   - Bahasa Melayu
   - Mandarin
   - Cantonese
   - Tamil
   - Hokkien
   - Other: (enable "Other")

### Section 3: Style and budget

10. **Which look do you like?** [Multiple choice, required]
    Help text: Examples of each are on our page.
    - Bright and soft (clean, airy, white tones)
    - Moody and cinematic (darker, richer colour)
    - Candid (natural moments, less posing)
    - Not sure, show me options

11. **Budget** [Multiple choice, required]
    - [TIER 1 NAME]: RM[___]
    - [TIER 2 NAME]: RM[___]
    - Not sure yet
    (Fill in once package pricing is decided.)

12. **Instagram or Pinterest photos you like (optional)** [Paragraph, optional]
    Help text: Paste links. Even one helps.

13. **Anything we should know?** [Paragraph, optional]
    Help text: Wheelchair access, a grandparent flying in, you hate being photographed, etc.

14. **How did you hear about us?** [Multiple choice, required]
    - Instagram
    - Threads
    - TikTok
    - A friend
    - Other: (enable "Other")

**Confirmation message:**

> Got it. We'll WhatsApp you two photographers who fit, usually within 2 days. A small deposit locks your date once you've picked one.
