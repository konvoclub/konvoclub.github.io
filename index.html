How it works

Three steps. No browsing.

  1. 01

    Tell us about your day

    Date, campus, budget, how many family members, and whether you like bright, moody or candid photos. Two minutes on a form.

  2. 02

    Get two photographers

    We WhatsApp you a shortlist of two from the Dean's Lens, picked for your style and your family's language. Not forty. Two.

  3. 03

    Pick one, pay one price

    One flat package price, told to you upfront. A small deposit locks your date. No haggling with the photographer, no surprise add-ons.

The Dean's Lens

A nice feed isn't enough.

The Dean's Lens is our list of screened photographers. Here's what every photographer goes through before we send them to you.

  • One full delivered gallery

    Every photo a real client received, not their highlights. We're checking that shot 80 is as good as shot 8.

  • Gear that won't let you down

    A proper body and lenses, plus a spare battery and spare card on every shoot. Your convo doesn't get a second take.

  • On-time delivery

    We ask how long they take and hold them to it. Late galleries are covered by our guarantee.

  • A call to see how they handle families

    Nervous grads, impatient uncles, aunties who want 15 more group shots. We talk to every photographer before they're listed.

Questions

Fair questions.

How much does it cost?

One flat package price, shown to you before you pay anything. No extra charge for the matching itself. A small deposit locks your date once you've picked your photographer.

Which campuses do you cover?

Anywhere in the Klang Valley: Sunway, Taylor's, Monash, UM, UKM, UiTM, UPM and the rest. Graduating outside the Klang Valley? Ask us. We can sometimes arrange it with a travel fee.

What if I don't like either photographer?

Tell us why and we'll send you different options. You don't pay anything until you've picked one.

Can my family join the photos?

Yes, and they usually should. Tell us how many people and what language they're most comfortable in, and we'll factor that into the match.

Who runs Konvo Club?

We're a small team from WaffleBox Productions, a photography studio in Puchong. WaffleBox is on the Dean's Lens under the same rules as everyone else.
