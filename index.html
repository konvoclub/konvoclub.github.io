Every photographer is on the Dean's Lens.

It's our list of photographers who've passed our checks. Before anyone gets on it, we look at:

  • Their portfolio, client work or practice shoots
  • Their gear, including spare batteries and memory cards
  • Whether they deliver on time
  • How they handle families on the day

If your photos are late or not what we agreed on, we'll reshoot or give you a partial refund.
